# Observation fairness boundary

Each adapter instance builds observations from its own Mineflayer client. Entity positions come from that client's entity map, blocks and line of sight come from its loaded voxel world, and the world border comes from border packets received on that same connection. Opponent and loot knowledge starts unknown and is current only while the chunks intersecting its 40-block perception range are loaded; a later gap makes the last sample stale. The observer applies a 120-degree horizontal field of view, a 90-degree vertical field of view, and voxel line of sight before reporting opponents or dropped items.

Self state stays unknown until the client has spawned, received health, and received a full player-inventory snapshot. Chest candidates come only from the local block world; a chest is marked opened after this client receives the corresponding container window. Skills recheck local target visibility before acting and wait for observed damage, collection, food, equipment, and window changes. Each pathfinder bot receives at most 3 ms of search time in a 10 ms worker window; queue time counts against its navigation deadline. Cancellation clears controls and restores the cursor and owned window after inventory actions settle. If an inventory action cannot settle within its bound, the client is quarantined, overlapping cleanup is skipped, and the cleanup failure is surfaced.

Core `navigate` keeps its two-block arrival radius. Bot-owned `navigateToBlock` uses Pathfinder's exact block goal and confirms that the local player position is inside the requested block before reporting success.
